Une vie bien rangée

A Tidy Life · a packing and life game

A whole life, told through the things we put away. Fit everything into the toy bin, the school bag, the locker… and grow up, puzzle by puzzle, from baby to 105.

Coming soon to Google Play

On Android, in English and French.

A place for every thing

Every object has its shape, every container has its corners. Rotate, slide, try again: when everything fits just right, it's the small joy of a tidy space.

  • Hundreds of increasingly clever puzzles.
  • Everyday objects, carefully illustrated, almost real.
  • No timer: play at your own pace.
  • Stuck? A hint shows you where the next object goes.

A life that grows with you

Between puzzles, life asks you questions. Go to the party or study? Really clean your room, or just close the door very calmly? Like a choose-your-own-adventure book, your choices matter.

  • Nine life stages, from baby to elder, with school, a first job and everything in between.
  • Your gauges unlock as you grow and shape your path.
  • Take care of your well-being: it affects how long your life lasts. No sudden bad luck, only the consequences of your choices.
  • Experience
  • Education
  • Well-being
  • Relationships
  • Money

A respectful game

  • No account needed: your game stays on your device.
  • No ads during the first stages of life.
  • Rewarded ads you choose to watch, at most 3 per day.
  • A one-time “No ads” purchase for peaceful play.
